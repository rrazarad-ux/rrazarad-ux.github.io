How Does a Flying Car Work?

A flying car by Zuma combines advanced electric vehicle technology with a smart aviation system. In driving mode, the vehicle operates like a modern electric car.

When switching to flight mode, wings and electric propellers deploy, allowing vertical takeoff similar to a controlled advanced drone.

The vehicle lands safely in designated areas, ensuring quiet operation, high efficiency, and maximum safety.
